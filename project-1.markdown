overview: In this project, I was tasked with writing a resume and cover letter geared towards a specific job description. I chose a Software Developer Internship position at Roblox because it is one of my top job choices. I have already applied to this internship in the past, and I plan to be persistent in the upcoming years.

approach: When writing my cover letter and resume, I made sure to include all standard components in those types of documents. For example, sender address, recipient address, and a salutation were all included in my cover letter. My resume also included conventional sections, such as education, experience, work, and skills. I already had a resume to improve upon before starting this project.

revisions: Formatting was one key issue with my resume in particular. I generally used bullets and indentation in inconsistent ways. Also, my excessive usage of bullets took up valuable space on the single-page document. In combination with the standard 1-inch margins and 12-point font, it was difficult to fit all the content that I needed to share. Restructuring my bulleted lists and indentation allowed me to free up more space for important information. My cover letter mainly faced issues regarding vague explanations. I initially left some explanations vague due to fear that my technical wording may not be understood correctly. Although I am applying to a technical position, the specific softwares and frameworks that I've used in my projects may not be completely in the knowledge of an employer readimg me resume. To revise this, I have strengthened these explanations while keeping the document on a single page. I have also revised the screenshot of my job posting by adding all relevant position information.

learning: In the current job market, it is conventional to apply to hundreds of jobs. Job application skills are a must-have, and speed is an important factor. Creating a parseable resume is especially useful, as most resumes are parsed by automated systems. This assignment has strengthened my ability to write an effective resume and cover letter. My current job search has also taught me that the quantity of job applications is extremely important today.
