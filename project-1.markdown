overview: In this project, I was tasked with writing a resume and cover letter geared towards a specific job description. I chose a Software Developer Internship position at Roblox because it is one of my top job choices. I have already applied to this internship in the past, and I plan to be persistent in the upcoming years.

approach: When writing my cover letter and resume, I made sure to include all standard components in those types of documents. For example, the sender's address, the recipient's address, and a salutation were all included in my cover letter. My resume also included conventional sections, such as education, experience, work, and skills. I already had a resume to improve upon before starting this project.

revisions: Formatting was one key issue with my resume in particular. I used bullets and indentation inconsistently. Also, my overuse of bullets took up valuable space on the single-page document. Together with the standard 1-inch margins and 12-point font, this made it difficult to fit all the content I needed to share. Restructuring my bulleted lists and indentation allowed me to free up more space for important information. My cover letter mainly faced issues with vague explanations. I initially left some explanations vague because I was afraid my technical wording might not be understood correctly. Although I am applying to a technical position, an employer reading my resume may not be familiar with the specific software and frameworks I've used in my projects. To revise this, I strengthened these explanations while keeping the document to a single page. I also revised the screenshot of my job posting by adding all relevant position information.

learning: In the current job market, it is conventional to apply to hundreds of jobs. Job application skills are a must-have, and speed is an important factor. Creating a parseable resume is especially useful, as most resumes are parsed by automated systems. This assignment has strengthened my ability to write an effective resume and cover letter. My current job search has also taught me that the number of job applications is extremely important today.
