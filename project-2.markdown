overview: In this project, our group of four was tasked with writing a research proposal that could be realistically pitched here at the University of Central Florida (UCF). We chose to analyze the potential benefits of utilizing artificial intelligence for student academic advising.

approach: The first step, and foundation of our project, was research. We researched peer-reviewed journal articles, gathering data about the successes and failures involved with implementing AI into academic advising. Next, we planned out our own research process that would build on top of existing research in the field. We included conventional proposal sections where we described the problem, solution, and budget, for example. We were able to cut down on research costs by utilizing free Copilot services provided by UCF, but this only partially covers the total AI expenses.

revisions: While this project was initially created in a group, revisions have been made individually. The key issue with our research proposal was a lack of explanation for our problem. Without strong proof of the flaws of academic advising, our research will be perceived as unneeded. To revise this, I strengthened the problem description with specific cited statistics. For example, the proposal now emphasizes the real average time needed to schedule advising appointments. Our initial proposal also faced feasibility issues. Specifically, it was unreasonable to expect that UCF would provide access to student data. To solve this issue, the research has been adjusted to rely on students manually importing their data.

learning: Writing a proposal has taught me about the benefits and feasibility of research. It is wonderful to see that universities provide funding to research projects. To fully utilize these types of opportunities, a proper research project must be designed, along with a clear research proposal. Encountering issues with the feasibility of my research proposal has helped me strengthen my skills for planning feasible research. Working in a team has also taught me team organization skills in terms of writing.
